# GDPR & Retention Plan

## Data Categories

- Public enquiries: name, email, organisation, enquiry type and message.
- Staff records: contact details, employment details, emergency contact and compliance data.
- Staff evidence: right-to-work, DBS, training and qualification files.
- Payroll records: rates, approved hours, expenses, deductions and payslips.
- Incidents: operational, first aid and safeguarding reports.

## Retention Defaults To Confirm

- Public enquiries: archive after 12 months if no active relationship exists.
- Staff records: retain for the legally required period after employment ends.
- Safer recruitment evidence: retain according to school/childcare compliance guidance.
- Payroll records: retain according to UK payroll and tax requirements.
- Safeguarding records: retain under safeguarding-specific advice and restrict access.

## Product Requirements

- Prefer archive/deactivate over hard delete.
- Add export tooling for subject access requests.
- Add audit logs for admin changes to staff, pay, documents, incidents and SCR checks.
- Use private storage for staff evidence, payslips and incident attachments.
- Use role-based access and stricter access for safeguarding-restricted records.
- Document lawful basis for each data category before production launch.
