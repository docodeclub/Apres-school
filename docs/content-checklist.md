# Content Checklist

## Homepage

- Confirm headline and subheading with the Après School team.
- Confirm parent and school CTAs.
- Confirm the key trust points are accurate.
- Confirm all imagery has permission for public use.

## Bookings

- Confirm every site name.
- Confirm whether each site is open to all children or school-specific.
- Confirm each booking platform link.
- Confirm age ranges, schedules and cancellation notes.
- Confirm the homepage booking dropdown sends each site to Magicbooking or Pebble correctly.

## Schools

- Add approved school partner testimonials when available.
- Add assurance-pack contents once confirmed.
- Add procurement or onboarding steps if schools request them often.

## Policies

- Add downloadable public policy PDFs when approved.
- Confirm privacy and terms wording with legal/compliance support.
- Keep safeguarding copy public-facing and avoid operational specifics.
- Confirm the assurance-pack request wording matches what can be supplied to schools today.

## Staff Application

- Confirm the onboarding form fields match the latest safer recruitment process.
- Confirm who reviews submitted applications before an account is created.
- Confirm which fields must become SCR evidence after approval.

## Contact

- Confirm enquiry notification inbox.
- Confirm response-time promise if the team wants one.
- Confirm recruitment enquiry routing.
